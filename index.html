FINISHED GOODS — MONTH-WISE COMPARISON SUMMARY

The monthly P&L In Ward Freight is allocated across all production CTN. Grocery and expense categories are allocated by production KG. Payroll is distributed automatically: 48% Direct, 23% Selling and 29% Administrative. In this Finished Goods comparison, Owner Payroll is included back in the same 48/23/29 split before calculating expense / CTN. R&D combines Research and Development + Sample. Forty percent of Electricity Bill Factory moves from Indirect to Storage. Unclassified / uncategorized P&L expenses are added to Indirect Expense using the actual positive monthly remainder; Government Tax and Owner Payroll remain excluded.

MONTHLY EXPENSE CATEGORY BREAKUP

First section shows Base Assembly Cost + Grocery + In Ward Freight. Expense categories follow. UNCATEGORIZED ADDED TO INDIRECT is included exactly once in Indirect Expense. TOTAL EXPENSE = P&L Total Expense − Owner Payroll. FINAL PRODUCTION COST = Base Assembly Cost + Grocery + In Ward Freight + Total Expense. Owner Payroll remains excluded.

MONTHLY BASE COST + P&L EXPENSE TOTAL

BASE COST TOTAL = Base Assembly Cost + Grocery + In Ward Freight. P&L TOTAL EXPENSE is verified from Gross Profit − Net Income for each month. TOTAL EXPENSE = P&L Total Expense − Owner Payroll. FINAL TOTAL = Base Cost Total + Total Expense.

POTATO YIELD — ITEM-WISE MONTH COMPARISON

Each item is shown once. Every month shows Production CTN, Potato Used KG, Potato KG / CTN and Yield %. Yield = Finished Output KG ÷ Potato Used KG × 100.
